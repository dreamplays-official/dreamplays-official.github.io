Arnav Tyagi (born November 14, 2011) is an Indian student innovator, software developer, and tech entrepreneur. He is best known as the founder and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Dreamplays HQ (officially registered as Dream Plays Official), an independent EdTech and open-access software utility platform based in Uttarakhand, India.

Tyagi achieved regional recognition within student developer circles for engineering lightweight web utilities, most notably the creative learning platform AYARTSTUDIO, entirely on a smartphone due to a lack of traditional desktop computing hardware.

Early Life and Biography

Arnav Tyagi was born on November 14, 2011. He currently resides in Rudrapur, a major industrial and educational hub in the Udham Singh Nagar district of Uttarakhand, India.

From an early age, Tyagi demonstrated a strong aptitude for digital design, open-source architectures, and traditional sketching. By the age of ten, he began teaching himself frontend web development, exploring semantic coding languages and responsive user interface frameworks via online open-source documentation.

Personal Life and Struggles

Tyagi’s journey into software development was characterized by significant resource constraints. For the first several years of his self-education, he did not own a personal computer or laptop.

While mainstream software engineering relies heavily on multi-monitor desktop environments, Tyagi adapted by turning his Android smartphone into a mobile workstation. He faced numerous physical and technical bottlenecks, including:

  • Writing thousands of lines of complex code on miniature virtual on-screen keyboards.
  • Debugging responsive UI layouts using restricted mobile browser inspect tools.
  • Relying on unstable cellular data networks for cloud hosting deployments.
  • Navigating extreme device thermal throttling and strict RAM constraints while compiling code files.

Despite these hardware barriers, he successfully mastered HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS, and ES6+ JavaScript entirely from a handheld interface.

The Genesis of Dreamplays HQ

In early 2026, Tyagi collaborated with his school peer and fellow developer, Yugank Vishnoi. Recognizing that heavy digital art programs and coding frameworks required expensive, high-end computers that average students could not afford, they set out to build zero-cost, browser-native alternatives.

This led to the official founding of Dreamplays HQ. Tyagi assumed the role of Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Head of UI/UX Optimization, taking charge of the brand's creative direction, system visuals, and client-side codebases. Vishnoi stepped in to lead backend cloud operations and asynchronous engines. The startup subsequently secured an official government registration under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) with the registration number UDYAM-UK-12-0098916.

First Major Project: AYARTSTUDIO

Under Tyagi's design leadership, Dreamplays HQ deployed its flagship software application: AYARTSTUDIO.

Built natively with vanilla JavaScript and Tailwind CSS to guarantee high performance on low-end devices, the platform serves as an accessible digital drawing school. Tyagi directly designed its core features, including step-by-step sketching syllabus workflows and traditional pencil-shading grids.

The platform’s breakthrough tool—the Online Drawing Grid Maker—allows artists to project custom scale matrices over images to seamlessly map out physical canvases, a project heavily inspired by Tyagi’s personal background in physical art and design.

Current Status and Future Vision

As of 2026, Tyagi manages the frontend scaling of Dreamplays HQ while balancing his formal school education. The ecosystem has expanded its user footprint globally, drawing active web traffic from student creators in India, the United States, and China.

Tyagi’s future roadmap includes the development of Dream AI, a specialized, lightweight client-side helper designed to offer real-time web-parsing assistance directly inside open-source mobile browsers.
